move. Pick by the frame's role. Prefer the one whose motion is closest to the

## Rules

- The visuals are invented: this explainer has no footage and no stock imagery.
  `focal` names the invented hero element — a hero word, a diagram node, a chart
  series. `roles` names the other invented elements.
- `scenes` is the frame's time-coded sequence. Pace the reveals to the spoken
  line so the frame keeps developing across its whole duration. A frame that
  finishes arriving in the first second and then holds still is wrong.
- `at_s` is seconds from the start of that frame, and the first is 0.
- One film-wide `current` direction. Every ordinary seam uses it; a different
  direction has to mean something, so do not alternate for variety.
- You write no HTML in this step.
